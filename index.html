Organization

This task force belongs to the Fuzzy Systems Technical Committee (FSTC) of the Computational Intelligence Society (CIS) of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, Inc. (IEEE).

Objectives

The main goal of this Task Force is to promote the understanding, research, development, and education in Fuzzy Control, both theory and applications. Therefore, it focuses on the dissemination of recent advances in Fuzzy Control in industry and academia. It also offers an opportunity for researchers and practitioners to identify new promising research directions.
