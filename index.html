About

The main goal of this Task Force is to promote the understanding, research, development, and education in fuzzy control, both theory and applications. It focuses on the dissemination of recent advances in fuzzy control in industry and academia, and offers an opportunity for researchers and practitioners to identify new promising research directions.

The Task Force belongs to the Fuzzy Systems Technical Committee (FSTC) of the IEEE Computational Intelligence Society (CIS). It was established in 2011.
